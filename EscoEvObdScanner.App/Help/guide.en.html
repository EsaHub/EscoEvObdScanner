Esco EV OBD Scanner

Reads an electric car's battery and fault codes through the OBD port. The program only reads: it does not write to the car, clear fault codes or unlock anything.

1. What you need

  • A Windows computer with the program installed.
  • An ELM327 or STN adapter that shows up in Windows as a COM port: over USB or classic Bluetooth. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE adapters do not work. Windows 11 offers a Bluetooth OBD adapter for pairing only when Advanced is chosen in Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Devices → Bluetooth devices discovery. Pairing creates two COM ports; use the outgoing one.
  • A supported car:
    PlatformCarsTested
    Stellantis e-CMPOpel Mokka-e, Corsa-e; Peugeot e-208, e-2008; Citroën e-C4; DS 3 E-Tense2023 Mokka-e
    Kia Niro EV (2022–)Second-generation Niro EV2024 Niro EV
    Kia e-Niro / Hyundai Kona Electric (2018–2022)First generationnot tested

The program also has a demo car that works without an adapter. Its data comes from a real car's readings, so everything can be tried at home.

The program was developed and tested with an OBDLink EX USB adapter, so that one is known to work. The identifier scans have also been run over Bluetooth with an OBDLink LX. Other adapters should work if they show up as a COM port, but they have not been tested.
The main difference between adapters: the adapter must handle long replies made of many frames and the flow control set-up commands (ATFCSH, ATFCSD, ATFCSM). The cell voltages come in such replies (on a Stellantis all 108 cells in one 243-byte reply). Without them the charge and voltages show, but the cell voltages do not. Test a new adapter on the Battery tab: if every cell shows, the adapter is fine.

2. The first time

  1. Plug the adapter into the car's OBD port (usually under the dashboard on the driver's side) and switch the car to READY.
  2. On the Connection tab press Add vehicle…. Give it a name, a registration number for example, and choose the right platform. The wrong platform is the most common reason nothing comes from the car.
  3. Choose the Port (the adapter's COM port). If it is missing, press ⟳.
  4. Press Connect. The log says "Connected", and the VIN if the car gives it.
  5. On the Dashboard tab press Start live view. The dashboard starts to update.
  6. Drive a round with the live view on, read the Fault codes (deep read) with the car standing, and finally press Create report (PDF) on the Battery tab.
Each vehicle has its own results and drive history. When you measure another car, add a vehicle for it, so the results do not mix. The program recognises the car and warns when the adapter is on another car than the vehicle chosen.

While the live view runs it holds the adapter. The actions of the tabs that read the car (Fault codes, ECUs, Discovery, Compare) wait, and while one of them is selected the bottom of the window shows a notice and a Stop live view button.

4. Live view and driving

  • The live view is recorded to a file, from which a report can be made later (Report from recording…).
  • Cells under load needs reads under load (accelerating, at least 40 A) and at rest (standing, at most 5 A). So drive with some variety and stop now and then.
  • The range estimate comes from the battery's remaining energy and the consumption: at the drive history's consumption (this drive included) once there are at least 30 km of drives, and at this drive's consumption after 5 km of driving. The program does not read the range estimate on the car's own dashboard.

5. Battery report

The report is made on the Battery tab. In Text for the report you can write whose car it is, for example, or the VIN if the car does not give it (on the tested e-CMP car the VIN cannot be read through the OBD port).

What the report holds

  • VIN: compare it with the lower corner of the windscreen and the registration document. This ties the report to the car measured.
  • Summary: ratings Good, Attention and Check.
  • Battery values, the drive, cells under load, cells and probes, fault codes, and the car's lifetime counters where the car gives them (Kia Niro EV: energy into and out of the battery, and charging; Stellantis: energy into and out of the battery, starts and time above 55 °C). Stellantis gives the energy into the battery only once the car has driven or charged since it woke; until then the row is left out.
  • Battery and battery management (Stellantis): the battery management's date of manufacture, the age worked out from it, the wear rate ((100 % − SOH) per year and per 10,000 km; assumes the new battery was at 100 % and is as old as its battery management) and the software and hardware versions. They are read at the connection and kept with the vehicle, so a report made from a recording has them too, with the date they were read.
  • 12 V battery: the voltage and whether it is a rest voltage (the car standing, pack current at most 5 A, below 13 V) or the DC/DC is charging the battery (13 V or more). The rest voltage references (full about 12.65 V, half about 12.24 V; Battery University BU-903 [6]) are indicative only: a reliable reading needs hours of rest.
  • The Source column tells for each figure whether it is given by the car as it is or calculated by the program from the car's values.
  • How the figures are calculated: the formula of every calculated figure.
  • Limits of the ratings: every rating's limits and their basis (see the next section).
The report is not a manufacturer's or workshop's battery test nor a guarantee of the battery's condition. A PDF can be edited: when a car is sold, the most reliable result is a measurement the buyer is present at.

7. Fault codes

  • Read fault codes reads the codes of the known ECUs. ECUs that have never answered this car are skipped unless Also ask ECUs that have not answered is chosen. Deep read also reads each fault's freeze frame and counters. On e-CMP cars the Occurred column also shows when, and at what odometer reading, the fault occurred.
  • active = the fault is on now, past, not now = the fault was seen before but is not on now.
  • Failure type (SAE J2012-DA) shows the code's last byte (e.g. U1208-29) by its name in the standard, for example 13 = Circuit Open, 29 = Signal Invalid. Values the maker defines have no name.
  • Status bits (ISO 14229-1) shows the bits of the status byte the car reports by their names in the standard, and Meaning the same bits in plain words, for example testFailed = failing now, confirmedDTC = confirmed fault, warningIndicatorRequested = asks for the warning light.
  • The program never clears fault codes.
  • Read fault codes with the car standing in READY. The live view holds the adapter, so stop it first.

8. Safety

  • The program sends read requests only. Commands that write, clear or unlock do not exist in it.
  • A long session keeps the car awake and drains the 12 V battery. Keep the car in READY or on charge, and unplug the adapter when you finish.
  • Some e-208 and Corsa-e owners report that an OBD adapter in use while driving upset the infotainment or the displays. If the car's screens behave oddly during the live view, stop it and unplug the adapter.
  • Do not use the program while driving yourself. Let a passenger handle the computer, or do things at stops.

9. Troubleshooting

SymptomCause and fix
No values come, and the ECUs "did not answer" (NO DATA in the adapter traffic)The wrong platform. Disconnect, remove the vehicle and add it again with the right platform.
Connect fails, the adapter does not answerThe wrong port or speed. Try 38400. Some adapters fall asleep while the car rests: unplug and plug in the adapter.
The port is busyAnother program uses the adapter. Close it.
Warning: the adapter is on another car than the vehicle chosenChoose the right vehicle or add a new one. If it surely is the same car (a replaced battery ECU, for example), answer Yes.
The report says "VIN: not given by the car"The car does not give its VIN through the OBD port (so on the tested e-CMP car). Write the VIN in Text for the report.
The cells do not show, other values doThe adapter does not handle long replies or the flow control commands (typical of cheap clones). Try another adapter.
The report's Cells under load says "The cells are not ranked"Too few reads under load or at rest. Drive longer, accelerate now and then and stop now and then.

10. Where the results are

Results go to Documents\EscoEvObdScanner\<vehicle>: reports, live recordings, fault code reads, snapshots and logs. Open folder on the Connection tab opens Documents\EscoEvObdScanner, where each vehicle has a folder of its own.

The files can hold ECU serial numbers and the car's VIN. Think about whom you share them with.
